QA Bench 0.2.0 is the first public release: an open benchmark for human-like QA of websites and web apps. It ships
local apps with planted and natural defects, a generator for episodes and sealed answer keys, a deterministic matcher
and an impact-weighted scorer. Scores are comparable only within one bench version, and 0.2.0 scores aren't
comparable with 0.1.0-b0.

## What's new since 0.1.0-b0
- **A harder bench.** Mixed regression episodes carry 2 or 3 planted bugs. The expense app now has 30 planted
  defects, 16 of them subtle. Labels record difficulty (subtle or blatant) and exposure (novel or known), and the report splits recall
  by both.
- **Exploration track (7.7).** Briefs give only the targets, the roles and a one-line purpose: no journeys, no
  checklist.
- **Visual track (7.3).** 7 stylesheet defects, each an in-place edit of a real rule, plus Swag Labs `visual_user`
  episodes and a deliberate restyle. The report adds visual and accessibility recall, and shows how often an agent
  took and looked at screenshots.
- **Natural defects.** Verified real bugs that nobody planted, each with a `verify.ts` check. When a planted defect
  hides one, the label says so (`masked_by`) and that episode's key leaves it out.
- **Traps.** Correct content that looks like a bug; reporting one counts as a false alarm. The matcher is now
  `deterministic-4`.
- **Judge calibration (7.9).** A pinned 60-case visual judge set and a capped judge harness.
- **Adapters.** A Claude agent harness (prompt, `qa-tester` subagent and transcript audit), deterministic page checks
  and a capped model-led hunt.

## Results in this release
Each leaderboard folder states its sample size, cost estimate and caveats.
- **Visual track** ([`v0.3-visual-agents`](../../leaderboard/v0.3-visual-agents/README.md), 12 episodes, 17
  planted defects): Claude Haiku 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5 at high effort found 17 of 17; at low effort they found 12 and 11.
  The scripted Playwright suite found 2.
- **Regression and exploration** ([`v0.2-agents`](../../leaderboard/v0.2-agents/README.md), 12 episodes): planted
  recall ranged from 35% to 100%. Subtle defects did most of the separating.
- **Judge calibration** ([`2026-11-09-judge`](../../leaderboard/2026-11-09-judge/README.md)), **hunt prompts**
  ([`touchstone-hunt`](../../leaderboard/touchstone-hunt/README.md)) and **page checks**
  ([`2026-11-10-touchstone-checks`](../../leaderboard/2026-11-10-touchstone-checks/README.md)).

Leaderboards run before this release record `0.2.0-dev`. They are unchanged.
